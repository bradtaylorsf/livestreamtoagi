# Public Website as Research Surface

This research note is backdated to 2026-04-10, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

How do I make the project inspectable to people who are not inside the dev environment?

## System Move

I added public pages for agents, world state, evals, conversations, challenges,
contributions, lore, safety, and simulations. The website started to turn internal
artifacts into an external research record.

## Evidence

- Layer 10 issues #56-#68 and continuation issues #326-#331 covered the public site work.
- Commits `be0141de`, `6fb512b7`, `14c4579b`, `61446f47`, `077e104a`, and `489fd1c1` wired real pages and APIs.
- `website/` and `website/src/lib/blog.ts` define the publishing surface.

## What Broke

A public site can make weak evidence look polished if the underlying artifacts are not
honest.

## Why It Matters

The signal is communication: I wanted outside readers to inspect runs, not just read
claims.

## What Changed Next

To make comparisons credible, simulation state had to be isolated.
