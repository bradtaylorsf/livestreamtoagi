# Civilization Mechanics Are Social Infrastructure

This research note is backdated to 2026-05-28, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

What does a Minecraft civilization need beyond more agents and more blocks?

## System Move

I added ownership ledgers, claim/release/list tools, trade and pricing, theft and
detection, diplomacy, alliances, factions, treaties, conflict categories, and scoring
hooks.

## Evidence

- E21 issues #891-#895 covered ownership, trade, theft, diplomacy, and conflict.
- Commits `6b934cf8`, `571b1210`, `b1d3cb03`, `a2e31af7`, and `d02f909e` landed the mechanics.
- `docs/minecraft/two-team-civilization-plan.md` and civilization mechanics code paths frame the social system.

## What Broke

Social mechanics can become bookkeeping unless agents actually use them to coordinate
meaningful work.

## Why It Matters

The signal is understanding that collaboration requires institutions, not just chat.

## What Changed Next

The task board became the next institution.
