# Replay Fidelity Is Research Fidelity

This research note is backdated to 2026-05-09, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

Can I trust a rendered artifact if it does not match the actual simulation?

## System Move

I fixed turn-level replay cues, real office rendering, roster fidelity, current port
targeting, fail-closed cue loading, MP4 status exposure, and visual regression checks.

## Evidence

- Epic 468/475 issues #462-#495 covered replay and MP4 export correctness.
- Commits `27752b3f`, `77bcf622`, `ea948328`, `a19e662c`, `dde34a60`, and `039d148b` landed the fixes.
- .alpha-loop summaries for epic 468 and 475 capture the replay fidelity learnings.

## What Broke

The first exports could look plausible while being wrong about turns, rosters, or
routes.

## Why It Matters

The signal is evidence discipline: visual output is not evidence unless it is faithful.

## What Changed Next

The same concern pushed the project toward a world where action could be independently
verified: Minecraft.
