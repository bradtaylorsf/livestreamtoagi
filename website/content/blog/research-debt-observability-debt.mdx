# Research Debt and Observability Debt

This research note is backdated to 2026-06-04, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

What debt appears when a research prototype becomes a long-running evidence machine?

## System Move

I consolidated observability, preserved artifact route lookup, tightened decision-log
fields, reviewed graph structure, and cleaned repo hygiene. The work was less visible
than Minecraft builds but directly tied to whether future findings are traceable.

## Evidence

## What Broke

Research debt appears when artifacts exist but readers cannot trace how decisions,
tools, models, and world changes connect.

## Why It Matters

The signal is maintenance maturity: observability is part of the research result.
