# The Repetition Problem

This research note is backdated to 2026-04-04, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

What happens when persistent agents have social machinery but not enough internal
pressure to change?

## System Move

The eval quality work made repetition visible. The transcripts showed repeated topics,
weak differentiation, identity bleed, missing speakers, and talk that did not reliably
turn into action.

## Evidence

- Eval `eval-e24db48d-1e96-4630-9456-72cc8f22f221.json` is the anchor artifact for early repetition concerns.
- Issues #213-#217 and #229-#236 tracked eval quality and repetition fixes.
- Commits `ef609af0` and `9dfc28b2` landed the first eval-quality fix session and completion.

## What Broke

The system could produce conversation that looked active while remaining behaviorally
stuck.

## Why It Matters

The signal is candor: the research notebook should publish negative results when they
are the actual finding.

## What Changed Next

Once repetition was measurable, the next question was how to judge social systems
without pretending the judge is perfect.
