# LLM-as-Judge Evals for Artificial Social Systems

This research note is backdated to 2026-04-04, the point in the repository
history when this part of the system became concrete enough to write about.

## Research Question

How do I evaluate open-ended agent behavior without reducing it to a single fake score?

## System Move

I built a set of eval categories for creativity, agency, productivity, social dynamics,
economic behavior, internal state, entertainment, safety, errors, dialogue quality,
narrative, and world evolution. The point was to organize findings and turn them into
implementation work.

## Evidence

- Issues #155-#158, #206-#212, and #240-#242 cover eval prompts, analysis UI, and the agency dimension.
- Commits `e364c03c`, `da05c8dd`, `c76b6c5c`, and `c809620a` landed eval categories and analysis work.
- `evals/prompts/*.yaml` and `evals/results/*.json` are the evaluation artifacts.

## What Broke

An LLM judge can reward fluent summaries and miss causal mechanisms. Every finding still
needs implementation evidence.

## Why It Matters

The signal is pragmatic methodology: noisy evaluators can still be useful when their
limits are explicit.

## What Changed Next

The same eval work also exposed that the safety role needed clearer framing.
